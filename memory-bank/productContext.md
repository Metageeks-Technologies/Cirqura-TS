## Project Goal

Develop a comprehensive website for Cirqura, a glass recycling company based in Dubai, UAE. The website will showcase the company's services in collecting and recycling glass bottles, PET bottles, aluminium cans, and other recyclable materials. It aims to promote sustainability, provide environmental benefits, and generate revenue through scrap sales and client services. The site will include educational content on recycling processes, challenges in the UAE market, and future goals to expand recycling efforts.

## Key Features

- **Recycling Services Overview**: Detailed sections on glass, PET, and aluminium collection and processing, including the 7-step recycling process (pre-treatment, sorting, crushing, mixing, melting, molding).
- **Sustainability Tracking**: App-based carbon footprint tracker for clients to monitor tonnage of materials collected and recycled.
- **Client Services**: Training and implementation support for achieving environmental goals, personalized sustainability data.
- **Educational Content**: Information on global and UAE recycling statistics, challenges like high collection costs, and benefits such as energy savings (40% less energy for recycled glass) and reduced CO2 emissions.
- **Market Insights**: Coverage of UAE trends, including growth in glass packaging for pharmaceutical, food & beverage, and personal care sectors, government policies like the 25 fils levy on plastic bags.
- **Contact and Business Info**: Contact details, business model explanation (revenue from beverage companies, scrap sales, training fees), and target customers (container glass manufacturers, flat glass manufacturers, cement manufacturers, EEG, Dubai Municipality, Tadweer).
- **Interactive Elements**: Real-time impact calculators, carbon footprint tracking dashboard, service area mapping for Dubai/UAE regions.
- **Client Testimonials**: Success stories and case studies showcasing partnership results and sustainability achievements.
- **Resources & Education**: Industry whitepapers, whitepapers, sustainability guides, market trend analysis, and thought leadership content.
- **Multi-language Support**: English and Arabic for UAE market accessibility.
- **Lead Generation**: Contact forms, consultation booking, resource downloads with business qualification.

## Overall Architecture

The website will be a dynamic Next.js application with the following main sections and pages:
- **Homepage/Hero**: Interactive hero with mission statement, real-time impact counter, service overview, and primary CTAs for consultation/quote requests.
- **About Us**: Company story, mission, team credentials, and Dubai operations focus.
- **Services**: Three-core service categories (Collection, Sustainability Solutions, Data & Analytics) with detailed value propositions.
- **Industries We Serve**: Targeted messaging for container glass manufacturers, flat glass manufacturers, cement manufacturers, and government partners.
- **Market Impact**: UAE market analysis, global projections, industry trends, and government policy alignment.
- **Sustainability Benefits**: Environmental and economic advantages with quantifiable metrics.
- **Process & Technology**: 7-step recycling methodology with technology integration details.
- **Resources & Education**: Whitepapers, sustainability guides, case studies, and thought leadership content.
- **Contact & Partnerships**: Multi-segment contact forms, consultation booking, and partnership inquiries.

Additional features include:
